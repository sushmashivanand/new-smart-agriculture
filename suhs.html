ABOUT SMART FARMING

What is Smart Agriculture?

Modern Technology Meets Agriculture 🌾

Smart agriculture is the use of modern technology and scientific methods to improve farming. It helps farmers use water, fertilizers, energy and other resources efficiently.

Technologies such as sensors, drones, GPS, artificial intelligence and automated irrigation systems can help farmers monitor crops and make better decisions.

✓ Efficient use of water
✓ Better crop monitoring
✓ Reduced resource wastage
✓ Sustainable farming

FARMING METHODS

Modern Farming Techniques

🌱

Organic Farming

Uses natural farming practices and reduces dependence on synthetic agricultural inputs.

💧

Drip Irrigation

Delivers water directly to plant roots and can improve water-use efficiency.

🌿

Hydroponics

Plants are grown without traditional soil using nutrient-rich water systems.

🚜

Precision Farming

Uses data and technology to manage crops according to field conditions.
